# Mandatory educational videos — 2026-11-27

Every new lesson must contain relevant educational video embeds, preferably two or more distinct instructional videos from authoritative sources. Verify source identity and relevance, use responsive Gutenberg embeds, inspect saved and rendered content, test actual playback and desktop/mobile layouts before claiming verification. Replace restricted, broken, irrelevant or duplicate videos. Static photographs, QR codes and bare video links do not satisfy the video requirement. If suitable video or playback verification is unavailable, disclose the specific blocker separately; never claim playback without evidence. During lesson patrols, audit older lessons for missing or defective video embeds and repair them. User directive: “Make sure the lessons have videos.”
