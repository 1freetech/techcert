<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>What Is an SSD?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>A <strong><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/04/15/ssd-vs-emmc/">Solid-State Drive</a> (SSD)</strong> uses <strong><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/04/10/ram-vs-flash-memoryssd-usb-memory-cards/">flash memory</a> chips</strong> to store data. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>There are no moving parts, making it <strong>faster, quieter, and more durable</strong> than traditional hard drives. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>SSDs have extremely fast read/write speeds, especially in <strong>NVMe (PCIe)</strong> form, which are ideal for boot drives, gaming systems, and business apps.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>SSDs come in different interfaces:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>SATA SSDs</strong> – Slower but compatible with older systems.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>NVMe SSDs</strong> – Use <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/06/peripheral-component-interconnect-express/">PCIe</a> lanes, offering speeds above 3000 MB/s.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>M.2 and U.2</strong> – Compact formats for modern systems.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>What Is an HDD?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>A <strong><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/04/09/ssd-vs-hdd/">Hard Disk Drive</a> (HDD)</strong> uses <strong>spinning magnetic platters</strong> and a moving read/write arm. It’s <strong>slower</strong>, but <strong>cost-effective</strong> for <strong>large storage needs</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>Common capacities range from <strong>500GB to 12TB</strong>, and they’re still used for backups, surveillance systems, and cold data storage.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":""} -->
<p>HDDs are more prone to mechanical failure due to moving parts and typically have slower boot times and file transfers.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
