OSETC.005 connects voltage, current, and resistance through Ohm’s Law.

## Core formulas
- V = I × R
- I = V ÷ R
- R = V ÷ I

## Example: current
24 V across 12 Ω: I = 24 ÷ 12 = 2 A.

## Video reference
SparkFun demonstrates voltage, current, resistance, and Ohm’s Law using a real resistor circuit and measurements.

## Example: resistance
24 V at 6 A: R = 24 ÷ 6 = 4 Ω.

## Technician exercise
1. 12 V across 6 Ω = 2 A.
2. 24 V at 3 A = 8 Ω.
3. 2 A through 10 Ω = 20 V.
4. Keep units with every result.
5. Compare calculations with appropriate measurements on a safe training circuit.

## OSET takeaway
Ohm’s Law gives technicians a practical way to compare expected circuit behavior with measured values.
