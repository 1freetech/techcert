<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Add-on cards, also known as expansion cards, are hardware components inserted into expansion slots on a motherboard—typically PCIe slots—to extend a server's functionality. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These cards enable specialized performance beyond the system’s native capabilities, including enhanced network connectivity, accelerated processing, and advanced storage management. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nhNlrB8yiSU\u0026amp;pp=ygUPZXhhcG5zaW9uIGNhcmRz","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Common types include GPU cards for high-performance computing, network interface cards (NICs) for increased bandwidth or redundancy, and RAID controller cards for managing storage arrays. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jca3fbGQpIg\u0026amp;pp=ygUPZXhhcG5zaW9uIGNhcmRz","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Host Bus Adapters (HBAs) connect servers to external storage networks, while FPGA or ASIC cards serve niche applications such as real-time data processing or Bitcoin mining. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">NVMe add-on cards are used to expand fast solid-state storage options, especially when default slots are limited. In data center environments—especially immersion-cooled sites—add-on cards must be vetted for thermal tolerance, fluid compatibility, and power draw to ensure operational integrity and uptime.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
