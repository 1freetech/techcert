OSETC.006 keeps electrical power simple.

## The one formula
For basic DC or resistive examples: P = V × I.

## Easy example
120 V × 10 A = 1,200 W.

## Video

## Watts to kilowatts
1 kW = 1,000 W. Therefore 1,200 W = 1.2 kW.

## Quick practice
1. 12 V × 5 A = 60 W.
2. 24 V × 10 A = 240 W.
3. 240 V × 10 A = 2,400 W = 2.4 kW.

## OSET takeaway
Power = voltage × current for the basic examples in this lesson. Watts tell you power; 1,000 watts equals one kilowatt.
