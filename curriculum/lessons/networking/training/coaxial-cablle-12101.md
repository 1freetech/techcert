<p>Coaxial cabling is a type of electrical <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/04/05/essential-guide-to-cable-types-features-and-uses-for-it-professionals/">cable</a> used to transmit high-frequency signals, commonly found in applications such as cable television, broadband internet, and radio communication. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Its structure includes a central conductor (typically copper) that carries the signal, surrounded by an insulating layer, a metallic shield to reduce electromagnetic interference (EMI), and an outer plastic jacket for protection. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This unique design minimizes signal loss and external interference, making it effective for long-distance transmission. For example, RG6 cables are widely used for television and internet connections due to their superior shielding, while RG59 cables are often used in CCTV systems. Coaxial cables are valued for their durability, reliability, and capacity to carry data with minimal distortion.</p>
