<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Britain’s <strong>National Grid Electricity Transmission</strong> agreed last week to buy roughly <strong>73 acres</strong> at Dorman Point on the Teesworks estate in northeast England for a proposed high-voltage substation intended to support major AI data-centre development. <a href="https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/national-grid-sparks-deal-pave-074400601.html">Sky News reported the agreement on November 1, 2026</a>; the project remains at the planning stage, not operational.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The company's <a href="https://www.nationalgrid.com/electricity-transmission/network-and-infrastructure/infrastructure-projects">Tees Dock project listing</a> describes a proposed <strong>400-kilovolt</strong> transmission facility near Middlesbrough. <a href="https://planning.redcar-cleveland.gov.uk/Planning/Display/R/2026/0711/OOM">Redcar and Cleveland's planning register</a> records a valid outline application submitted September 24, with consultation open until November 11. Land purchase and planning consent are separate steps.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">A proposed 400-kV connection</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The former steelworks site requires remediation before construction. The proposed substation would connect industrial customers to Britain's transmission system, making grid capacity available to a nearby planned data-centre campus. A substation routes and controls electricity; it does <strong>not</strong> generate new electricity. This matters as UK connection delays have already affected <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/23/microsoft-exits-nscale-50mw-ai-data-center-uk-grid-delays-2030s/">other AI infrastructure projects</a>.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>National Grid describes <strong>more than 1 gigawatt of contracted import/export capacity</strong> in its project information, according to <a href="https://tsnmedia.org/teesside-substation-national-grid-dorman-point/">a review of its project documents</a>. Tees Valley mayor Ben Houchen separately cited <strong>up to 3.2 GW</strong> of potential site capacity. The figures have different scopes: neither establishes an approved 3.2-GW substation or an operating power plant.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Capacity claims need context</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The engineering scope involves high-voltage switching, protection, connection planning and potentially associated private-wire and battery-storage infrastructure. The challenge resembles the equipment demands behind <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/31/ge-vernova-trench-group-9-billion-765kv-grid-equipment-2026/">investment in high-voltage switchgear</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/31/idaho-lab-230kv-transformer-seismic-earthquake-test-2026/">transformer reliability</a>. It is not yet a confirmed construction contract for any named contractor.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The mayor says the new connection could position Teesside for a data-centre campus approaching 500,000 square metres. <a href="https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/nov/01/national-grid-to-site-biggest-substation-on-teesside-near-planned-ai-datacentre">The Guardian</a> reports that the project could become Britain's largest substation, but that description remains a projected comparison rather than an operating record. A final data-centre operator and delivery timetable have not been publicly confirmed.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">What happens next</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Next are planning decisions, land remediation, detailed engineering and regulatory review. The proposal follows wider efforts to supply high-density computing through <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/31/siemens-microgrid-integrated-switchboard-us-2026/">integrated power distribution</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/31/top-10-largest-data-center-energy-projects-america-2026/">large energy infrastructure</a>. For now, the milestone is land access—not energized grid capacity.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
