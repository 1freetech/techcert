# OSFOTC.001: Fiber Optic Safety, Handling, Inspection, and Cleaning Basics

The first Open Source Fiber Optics Technician lesson establishes the handling discipline required before technicians progress into loss testing, polarity, OLTS/OTDR work, splicing, and fault isolation.

## Core outcomes

Learners should be able to avoid direct viewing of unknown optical sources; control glass-fiber scraps; identify a circuit before disturbing it; protect connector ferrule endfaces; use an inspect-clean-inspect workflow; preserve dust-cap protection without treating caps as proof of cleanliness; respect manufacturer bend-radius and pulling-tension limits; recognize how contamination and bending can affect attenuation and insertion loss; use VFLs safely; and document patch-panel work.

## Technician workflow

```text
identify circuit
      ↓
make work area safe
      ↓
verify source state
      ↓
protect eyes and hands
      ↓
inspect
      ↓
clean if needed
      ↓
inspect again
      ↓
connect and route without stress
      ↓
label, test, document
```

## Technician rule

**Never look into unknown fiber. Control glass scraps. Inspect, clean if needed, inspect again, and respect the cable manufacturer's handling limits.**
