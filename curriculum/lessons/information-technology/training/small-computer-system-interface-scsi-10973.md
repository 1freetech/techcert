<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCSI">Small Computer System Interface</a> (SCSI), pronounced "scuzzy," is a set of standards for connecting and transferring data between computers and peripheral devices. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Introduced in the 1980s, SCSI became a prevalent interface for hard drives, scanners, printers, and other peripherals, especially in high-performance and server environments. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Despite the advent of newer interfaces like USB and FireWire, SCSI <a href="https://www.lifewire.com/why-scsi-still-matters-8730745">remains relevant</a> in business and enterprise server environments. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Its ability to manage multiple device connections efficiently and support high data transfer rates has ensured its continued use in scenarios where robust performance is critical. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Over the years, SCSI has evolved to meet the demands of modern computing. Developments such as Serial Attached SCSI (SAS) have provided faster data transfer speeds and improved reliability, making SCSI-based systems adaptable to contemporary requirements. ​</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The enduring <a href="https://www.techtarget.com/searchstorage/definition/SCSI">presence</a> of SCSI in server technology underscores its robustness and adaptability. As businesses continue to rely on legacy systems alongside modern infrastructures, SCSI's role in ensuring seamless data management and peripheral connectivity remains significant. </p>
