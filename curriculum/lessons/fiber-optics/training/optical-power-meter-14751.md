<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>An optical power meter is a precision instrument used in fiber optic networks to quantify the optical signal power transmitted through a fiber. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It operates by converting incoming light—usually in the infrared range—into an electrical signal using a calibrated photodetector, then displaying the power level in decibel-milliwatts (dBm) or milliwatts (mW). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r0XtKAtuSAk","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This measurement is critical for verifying signal levels during installation, maintenance, and troubleshooting of fiber links, ensuring that transmitters and receivers are operating within acceptable thresholds.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><br>The meter typically includes a wavelength-selectable interface to match the operating wavelength of the system (e.g., 850 nm, 1310 nm, 1550 nm), and may be paired with a light source to perform insertion loss testing. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Advanced models can store readings, interface with software for reporting, and support auto-wavelength detection. Accurate power measurements help prevent signal degradation, optimize link budgets, and confirm compliance with network specifications. Optical power meters are indispensable tools for technicians working in telecom, data centers, and enterprise fiber deployments</p>
