# Open Source Semiconductor Technician Certification (OSSTC)

**Rotation key:** `semiconductor_technician`  
**Status:** active  
**Starting lesson:** `OSSTC.001`

Hands-on semiconductor technician lessons covering fab safety, ESD control, cleanroom behavior, wafer handling, vacuum systems, gas delivery, process tools, metrology basics, preventive maintenance, fault isolation, and production support.

Use filenames in the form `lesson-001-<topic-slug>.md`. Technician lessons emphasize safe execution, measurements, procedures, tool response, and troubleshooting before deeper device/process design theory.

## Lessons

1. [OSSTC.001: Semiconductor Fab Cleanroom, Contamination, and ESD Basics](lesson-001-semiconductor-fab-cleanroom-contamination-esd-basics.md) — cleanroom discipline, contamination control, ESD/ESA, wafer handling, fab hazards, interlocks, and technician pre-work checks.
2. [OSSTC.002: Wafer Handling, FOUPs, and Automated Material Flow Basics](https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/04/osstc-002-wafer-handling-foups-automated-material-flow-basics/) — wafer carriers, load ports, EFEM transfer, mapping, AMHS, traceability, and safe wafer recovery.
3. [OSSTC.003: Semiconductor Vacuum Systems — Roughing Pumps, Turbomolecular Pumps, Gauges, and Leak Checks](https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/05/osstc-003-semiconductor-vacuum-systems-roughing-pumps-turbomolecular-pumps-gauges-leak-checks/) — fab vacuum architecture, pumps, gauges, pumpdown behavior, leak detection, and technician troubleshooting.
4. [OSSTC.004: Semiconductor Gas Delivery Systems — MFCs, Regulators, Valves, Purge, and Interlocks](https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/06/osstc-004-semiconductor-gas-delivery-systems-mfc-regulators-valves-purge-interlocks/) — gas delivery hardware, purge logic, leak checks, interlocks, and technician fault isolation.
5. [OSSTC.005: Semiconductor Pneumatic Systems](https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/06/osstc-005-semiconductor-pneumatic-systems-cda-regulators-solenoid-valves-cylinders-sensors-troubleshooting/) — CDA, regulators, solenoid valves, cylinders, sensors, and troubleshooting.
6. [OSSTC.006: Semiconductor RF Power Delivery](https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/07/osstc-006-semiconductor-rf-power-generators-matching-networks-forward-reflected-plasma-troubleshooting/) — RF generators, matching networks, forward/reflected power, and plasma troubleshooting.
7. [OSSTC.007: Wafer Probe Testing](https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/11/osstc-007-wafer-probe-testing-probe-stations-probe-cards-iv-cv-yield-troubleshooting/) — probe stations, probe cards, contact, IV/CV, yield maps, and troubleshooting.
8. [OSSTC.008: Semiconductor Metrology and Inspection Basics](https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/12/osstc-008-semiconductor-metrology-inspection-basics/) — measurement, inspection, overlay, focus, defect review, and process feedback.
9. [OSSTC.009: Photolithography Equipment Basics](../../archive/2026/10/24297-osstc-009-photolithography-equipment-basics.md) — coat/develop tracks, photoresist, reticles, alignment, focus, overlay, wafer stages, and technician fault isolation.
