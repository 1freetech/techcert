## Publishing rules

- Verify live WordPress numbering and GitHub duplicates before every lesson.
- Write lessons as formal instruction/lecture material, not author-to-reader conversation.
- Include an Elementary Overview at the beginning and an Elementary Conclusion at the end of every complex lesson.
- Do not use explanatory text boxes or arrow-style procedure/sequence diagrams.
- Use one unique realistic or otherwise approved rich-style 1200×630 cover; do not use minimalist covers and keep the cover out of the body.
- Every substantive paragraph must have at least one directly relevant verified playable video; never duplicate a video URL.
- BitcoinVersus internal-wiki linking has **no hyperlink limit**: whenever a meaningful hardware component, software item, protocol, company, technical keyword, person, standard, or concept has a relevant BitcoinVersus.Tech article/category, hyperlink that term to the most specific relevant BitcoinVersus.Tech page. Apply this throughout the entire lesson, including repeated meaningful terms where useful.
- Link earlier electrical lessons whenever they materially support the topic.
- Archive the exact final Gutenberg source to BitcoinVersus, TechCert, and Open CERT and read all three back before advancing rotation.
