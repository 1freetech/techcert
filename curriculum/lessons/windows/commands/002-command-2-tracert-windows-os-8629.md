<p style="text-transform:none"><br>The 'tracert' command, short for 'Trace Route,' is a network diagnostic tool that tracks the path data packets take from your device to a specified IP address or domain. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">By listing each hop and measuring the time it takes to travel between them, 'tracert' helps identify delays or connection issues along the route. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">For instance, executing 'tracert example.com' reveals the complete path packets follow to reach the 'example.com' server, aiding in troubleshooting network bottlenecks and routing problems.</p>
