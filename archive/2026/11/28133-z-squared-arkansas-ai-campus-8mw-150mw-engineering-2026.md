<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Z Squared said on October 30, 2026, that it has begun site planning and preliminary engineering at its Union County Campus in <strong>El Dorado, Arkansas</strong>. The company engaged A2 Advisors to help design construction phases, coordinate vendors and pursue tenants for an AI-ready data-center expansion. The property has <strong>approximately 8 megawatts of existing utility service</strong>, while its longer-term ambition reaches <strong>up to 150 MW</strong>.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The important distinction is that the larger capacity <strong>is not contracted, energized or delivered</strong>. The existing 8 MW connection is an interruptible utility arrangement, not evidence that 8 MW of AI servers are operating. Z Squared's <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/z-squared-begins-site-planning-and-engineering-at-energized-union-county-campus-in-el-dorado-arkansas-302924495.html">October 30 announcement</a> explicitly conditions expansion on more power arrangements, permits, financing, construction and tenant commitments.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Existing service is not completed AI capacity</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Z Squared acquired Paradox Data, owner of the site, in a transaction that closed September 8. Its <a href="https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1759186/000118518526003984/zsqrex99-1.htm">September disclosure filed with the SEC</a> describes the grid connection. Earlier transaction documents identify existing infrastructure for <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/15/top-7-data-center-cooling-technologies-in-2026/">immersion-cooled computing</a>, although no verified AI tenant deployment or hardware occupancy was established by the October update.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Engineers now need to turn the initial connection into a practical campus plan. That means specifying rack loads, electrical distribution, cooling systems, building footprints and phased construction before capacity can be marketed as ready for tenants. As our <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/29/osdcec-014-data-center-capacity-planning-demand-forecasting-power-headroom-infrastructure-growth/">data-center capacity-planning reference</a> explains, available utility power is not the same as usable IT load after conversion losses, redundancy and cooling demands.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":28123,"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} --><figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/z-squared-arkansas-union-county-8mw-existing-vs-150mw-target-2026-chart.png" alt="Source-backed comparison of Z Squared's 8 MW existing utility service and proposed future 150 MW AI-ready capacity, which is not contracted or energized" class="wp-image-28123" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption"><em>Source: <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/z-squared-begins-site-planning-and-engineering-at-energized-union-county-campus-in-el-dorado-arkansas-302924495.html">Z Squared, October 30, 2026</a>. The 8 MW is existing interruptible electric service; 150 MW is a long-term development goal, <strong>not contracted capacity or installed IT power</strong>. <!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The chart compares the company's existing electrical service with its future target. It is <strong>not a construction-progress chart</strong>: 150 MW remains a goal, and the two figures do not establish energized data-center floor space.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Planning for tenants and thermal systems</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>A2 Advisors will work on site layouts, technical partner coordination and leasing strategy. Z Squared says it has heard from prospective tenants about requirements for new AI hardware, but it disclosed <strong>no definitive lease</strong> in the release. A site plan is therefore an engineering and commercial milestone, not a commissioning announcement.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":28126,"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} --><figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/ai-data-center-immersion-cooling-plant-e28094-conceptual-technical-illustration.png" alt="Original concept of immersion-cooled compute equipment, heat exchanger, pumps and dry cooler, not a verified image of Z Squared's Arkansas site" class="wp-image-28126" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption"><em>Original generic technical illustration of immersion-cooling infrastructure. Z Squared's acquisition documents identify cooling assets at the property, but this image does not represent actual installed equipment or a disclosed engineering design.</em></figcaption></figure><!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The project is separate from the larger Arkansas campuses featured in BitcoinVersus.Tech's <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/27/top-3-largest-arkansas-data-center-projects-2026-full-report/">state data-center ranking</a> and from <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/11/01/entergy-google-driver-jones-mill-350mw-1400mwh-battery-filing-2026/">Entergy's Google-related battery filing</a>. Neither proves power availability for this property.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The next proof points are signed utility service, approved site plans, funded equipment orders, binding leases and commissioned compute capacity. Until then, the Arkansas campus is an <strong>energized starting point with an ambitious expansion proposal</strong>—not a completed 150 MW AI facility.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
