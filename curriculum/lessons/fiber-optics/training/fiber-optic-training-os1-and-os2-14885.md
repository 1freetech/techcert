<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>OS1 and OS2 are classifications of singlemode fiber optic cable defined by their construction and performance characteristics. OS stands for "Optical Singlemode."</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>OS1 is typically used for indoor applications and features tight-buffered construction, supporting up to 10 km at 1310 nm and 40 km at 1550 nm. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>OS2 is optimized for outdoor and long-haul deployments, using loose-tube or blown fiber construction with lower attenuation, supporting distances beyond 100 km. Both types use a 9 µm core, but OS2 offers superior performance in high-bandwidth, long-distance networks such as metropolitan or backbone infrastructures.</p>
