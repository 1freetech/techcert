# OSDCTC.001: Data Center Floor Fundamentals — Racks, Power, Cooling, Networking, and Safety

The first Open Source Data Center Technician lesson maps the physical data-center floor before deeper rack-and-stack, cabling, server, power, cooling, monitoring, and troubleshooting work.

## Core outcomes

Learners should be able to identify rack components and U positions; trace the high-level electrical path from utility/generator through switchgear, UPS, distribution, rack PDU, and server PSU; explain A/B power paths and reduced redundancy; recognize air, direct-to-chip liquid, rear-door, and immersion cooling; explain hot-aisle/cold-aisle airflow; identify switching, routing, VLAN, NIC, copper, and fiber dependencies; treat monitoring alarms as evidence rather than automatic root cause; and perform a structured technician walkdown before changing hardware.
