# OSRTC.001: Industrial Robot Safety — E-Stops, Safeguarding, and Safe Workcell Entry

A robotics technician's first responsibility is controlling hazardous motion and energy before learning routine robot movement. This lesson establishes the safety foundation for the Open Source Robotics Technician Certification track.

## Core outcomes

By the end of this lesson, a learner should be able to distinguish the robot work envelope from the safeguarded space; identify impact, crush, electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic, gravity and process hazards; explain why an emergency stop is not a substitute for safeguarding or energy isolation; describe interlocked gates, light curtains, scanners and other presence-sensing functions; distinguish emergency, safeguard/protective and ordinary operational stops at a conceptual level; explain manual/teach mode, reduced motion and three-position enabling devices; apply a safety-first workcell-entry thought process; and understand why reset is not the same as restart.

## Technician rule

**Stopped does not automatically mean safe.** Before entering a robot cell, determine what initiated the stop, what hazardous energy remains, what can cause motion to resume, and what verified safe state the task requires.
