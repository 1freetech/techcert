<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This guide outlines the essential components and step-by-step procedure for establishing an RS-485 serial connection using Ethernet and serial wiring. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It is intended for technicians integrating RS-485 devices with a Mikrotik router and a metering system above a breaker panel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>1. Here are the tools you will need to set up a RS 485 Device:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Miniature flathead screwdriver&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":14771,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-1-5.png?w=104" alt="" class="wp-image-14771" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>- Nuts and bolts that are compatible with black cabinet (server rack) </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":15082,"width":"231px","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-6.png?w=115" alt="" class="wp-image-15082" style="width:231px;height:auto" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>- Crimper for cutting Cat 5 Eth Cable&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":14766,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-1.png?w=98" alt="" class="wp-image-14766" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>- Two RS-485 Devices</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":14767,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-1-1.png?w=66" alt="" class="wp-image-14767" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":14770,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-1-4.png?w=66" alt="" class="wp-image-14770" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>- Ethernet cable&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":14768,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-1-2.png?w=89" alt="" class="wp-image-14768" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>- Red and Black Serial Wire&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":14769,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-1-3.png?w=93" alt="" class="wp-image-14769" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>2. Crimp the ethernet wire and isolate the blue-white striped wire and blue wire</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":15083,"width":"164px","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-7.png?w=105" alt="" class="wp-image-15083" style="width:164px;height:auto" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>3. Strip the Ethernet of its insulation covering so that the copper wire is showing. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":15085,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-8.png?w=115" alt="" class="wp-image-15085" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>4. Loosen <strong><em>Port A </em></strong>and <strong><em>Port B </em></strong>on the green attachment</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":14773,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-1-7.png?w=123" alt="" class="wp-image-14773" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>5. Place the Striped blue and white wire in <strong><em>Port A </em></strong>and the solid blue wire in <strong><em>Port B </em></strong><strong><em></em></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":15087,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-9.png?w=165" alt="" class="wp-image-15087" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>6. Next, grab the 2nd RS-485 detach the green module and loosen <strong><em>Port A </em></strong>and <strong><em>Port B </em></strong>RS-485 from the attachment&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":14772,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-1-6.png?w=90" alt="" class="wp-image-14772" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>7. Next Grab your red And black serial wires (the other end should be connected to the meter directly above the Breaker Panel)</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":14774,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-1-8.png?w=124" alt="" class="wp-image-14774" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>8. Place the red wire in <strong><em>Port A </em></strong>and the black wire in <strong><em>Port B</em></strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":14776,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-1-10.png?w=91" alt="" class="wp-image-14776" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>9. Connect the Ethernet cable from the Mikrotik to the RS-485. The RS-485 device should have a corresponding number for the port (port 17 or port 18) connect RS 485 #17 to port 17 and RS485 #18 to port 18.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":14775,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-1-9.png?w=77" alt="" class="wp-image-14775" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":14780,"width":"254px","height":"auto","sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><img src="https://bitcoinversus.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/screenshot-2025-11-02-162142.png?w=254" alt="" class="wp-image-14780" style="width:254px;height:auto" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Congratulations! You’ve just setup your RS-485 serial connection.</p>
