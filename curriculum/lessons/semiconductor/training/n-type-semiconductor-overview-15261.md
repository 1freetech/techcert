<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>An n-type semiconductor is created by doping a pure <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/09/21/intel-and-nvidia-join-forces-5b-deal-for-custom-cpus-gpus-ai-stack/">semiconductor</a>, such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2024/01/28/smarter-together-u-s-china-breakthrough-in-graphene-semiconductor-technology/">silicon</a>, with pentavalent elements like phosphorus, arsenic, or antimony. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>These dopants have five valence electrons, one more than silicon, and the extra electron becomes loosely bound and available for conduction. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As a result, electrons become the majority charge carriers in n-type materials, while holes are the minority carriers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The presence of these free electrons significantly increases the conductivity of the semiconductor compared to its pure form. N-type semiconductors are widely used in electronic devices, particularly in forming p–n junctions that are essential for diodes and transistors. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Their behavior under applied voltage allows them to control current flow and amplify signals. By carefully selecting dopants and concentrations, engineers can tailor the properties of n-type semiconductors to meet specific technological needs, making them indispensable in modern electronics.</p>
