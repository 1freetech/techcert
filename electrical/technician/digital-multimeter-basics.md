# Electrical Engineering Tech Doc #1 – Digital Multimeter Basics

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A digital multimeter is a tool used to measure electrical values. For a beginner, the three measurements to recognize first are <strong>voltage</strong>, <strong>current</strong>, and <strong>resistance</strong>.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Think of It as Three Tools in One</h2>
<ul class="wp-block-list"><li><strong>Voltage (V):</strong> electrical potential difference between two points.</li><li><strong>Current (A):</strong> electrical current flowing through a circuit.</li><li><strong>Resistance (Ω):</strong> opposition to current flow.</li></ul>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You do not need to master every meter setting today. The goal of this lesson is simply to recognize these three measurements and the basic parts of the meter.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Basic Parts of a Digital Multimeter</h2>
<ul class="wp-block-list"><li><strong>Display:</strong> shows the measurement.</li><li><strong>Dial:</strong> selects what you want to measure.</li><li><strong>COM jack:</strong> commonly receives the black test lead.</li><li><strong>Measurement jack:</strong> the red lead is placed in the correct jack for the measurement being made.</li><li><strong>Test leads:</strong> connect the meter to the points being measured.</li></ul>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Simple Battery Example</h2>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A small battery is a familiar place to understand voltage measurement. A digital multimeter can measure the voltage between the battery terminals when the meter and leads are configured correctly.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why Technicians Use Multimeters</h2>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Multimeters are used for installation, maintenance, repair, and troubleshooting. The same basic tool can help a technician check a battery, inspect an electronic circuit, or investigate electrical equipment.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Safety Comes First</h2>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Electrical measurement can be hazardous. Use equipment rated for the work, follow the meter manufacturer&#8217;s instructions, and follow applicable workplace electrical-safety procedures. Beginners should not practice on energized mains, panels, switchgear, or other hazardous equipment without appropriate training and supervision.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A digital multimeter combines several electrical measurement functions in one tool. Start by recognizing voltage, current, resistance, the display, the dial, the jacks, and the test leads.</p>
