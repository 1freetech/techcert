A digital multimeter is a tool used to measure electrical values. For a beginner, the three measurements to recognize first are voltage, current, and resistance.

Think of It as Three Tools in One

  • Voltage (V): electrical potential difference between two points.
  • Current (A): electrical current flowing through a circuit.
  • Resistance (Ω): opposition to current flow.

You do not need to master every meter setting today. The goal of this lesson is simply to recognize these three measurements and the basic parts of the meter.

Basic Parts of a Digital Multimeter

  • Display: shows the measurement.
  • Dial: selects what you want to measure.
  • COM jack: commonly receives the black test lead.
  • Measurement jack: the red lead is placed in the correct jack for the measurement being made.
  • Test leads: connect the meter to the points being measured.

Simple Battery Example

A small battery is a familiar place to understand voltage measurement. A digital multimeter can measure the voltage between the battery terminals when the meter and leads are configured correctly.

Why Technicians Use Multimeters

Multimeters are used for installation, maintenance, repair, and troubleshooting. The same basic tool can help a technician check a battery, inspect an electronic circuit, or investigate electrical equipment.

Safety Comes First

Electrical measurement can be hazardous. Use equipment rated for the work, follow the meter manufacturer's instructions, and follow applicable workplace electrical-safety procedures. Beginners should not practice on energized mains, panels, switchgear, or other hazardous equipment without appropriate training and supervision.
