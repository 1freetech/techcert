<p>Reflection occurs when an optical signal traveling through a fiber encounters a change in refractive index—typically at a connector, mechanical splice, or fiber break—and part of the light is reflected back toward the transmitter. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This backward-propagating light can interfere with the source, especially in systems using lasers, and may degrade signal quality or cause instability. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The magnitude of reflection depends on the surface finish, alignment, and presence of anti-reflective measures like angled physical contact (APC) connectors. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Reflection is a physical phenomenon, while Return Loss is its quantitative expression.</p>
