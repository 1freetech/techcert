# Electrical Engineering Tech Doc #1 – Digital Multimeter Basics

A digital multimeter measures electrical values. Begin by recognizing voltage, current, and resistance.

## Three Basic Measurements

- **Voltage (V):** electrical potential difference.
- **Current (A):** electrical current flowing through a circuit.
- **Resistance (Ω):** opposition to current flow.

## Basic Meter Parts

- Display
- Dial
- COM jack
- Measurement jack
- Test leads

## Simple Battery Example

A small battery is a familiar way to understand voltage measurement. A properly configured digital multimeter can measure voltage between its terminals.

## Why Technicians Use Multimeters

Multimeters are used for installation, maintenance, repair, and troubleshooting.

## Safety

Electrical measurement can be hazardous. Use properly rated equipment, follow manufacturer instructions and applicable workplace safety procedures, and do not practice on hazardous energized equipment without appropriate training and supervision.
