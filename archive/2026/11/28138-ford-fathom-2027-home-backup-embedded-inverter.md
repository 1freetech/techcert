<!-- wp:paragraph --><p><strong>Ford Motor Company said October 27, 2026</strong>, that its upcoming <strong>2027 Fathom electric pickup</strong> will house a bidirectional power inverter inside the vehicle, avoiding the separate wall-mounted inverter needed by earlier Ford home-backup systems. The truck is advertised from <strong>$28,350 MSRP</strong> and is projected to deliver <strong>more than 10 kilowatts</strong> of alternating-current power. Neither its customer installations nor the claimed output have yet been independently measured in production vehicles.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">The inverter moves into the truck</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>An EV battery stores direct-current energy, but household circuits use alternating current. Ford's new <strong>E-box</strong> integrates the conversion equipment within the truck, one of five principal modules in its Universal EV Platform. Ford's <a href="https://www.fromtheroad.ford.com/us/en/articles/2026/bidirectional-evs-scale-utility-interconnection">engineering explanation</a> distinguishes this design from the former <strong>F-150 Lightning</strong>, which depended on external conversion hardware. The new truck still needs compatible equipment installed at home.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

It illustrates the planned vehicle-side integrated electronics, not a finished residential installation. © Ford Motor Company.</em></figcaption></figure><!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Ford offers two proposed configurations: <strong>Home Power Management</strong> would shift electricity between the battery and home during different utility-rate periods; <strong>Home Backup Power</strong> would add an isolation/backup panel to supply the home during outages. The <a href="https://www.fromtheroad.ford.com/us/en/articles/2026/fathom-ev-bidirectional-home-power">company estimates</a> its new management station will cost about <strong>50% less</strong> than its earlier charger and its backup hardware package at least <strong>60% less</strong> than its predecessor. Final purchase and installation prices remain unpublished.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">Output, runtime and electrical safety</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>The distinction between <strong>power</strong> and <strong>energy</strong> matters. Ten kilowatts describes an instantaneous output rate; battery capacity in kilowatt-hours, household loads and reserve settings determine how long a truck could supply electricity. Ford has not released a verified Fathom-specific household runtime, so no number of outage days can be guaranteed.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

A firsthand demonstration of the older F-150 Lightning setup, presented for comparison; it is <strong>not</strong> a working Fathom installation.</em></p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Ford says the platform is designed around <strong>UL 1741</strong> and <strong>IEEE 1547</strong> interconnection requirements, using SAE J3068 communications. Its utility group includes AEP, BC Hydro, Consumers Energy, Exelon and PG&amp;E. Meeting design objectives does not itself establish completed certification or approval by every utility. The approach resembles wider <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/04/energy-germany-turns-ev-batteries-into-grid-assets-with-new-v2g-rules/">vehicle-to-grid efforts</a> and <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/04/tesla-sunrun-california-home-batteries-580-mw-virtual-power-plant/">virtual power plants</a>, though Fathom's utility exports remain planned.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading --><h2 class="wp-block-heading">A promised 2027 rollout</h2><!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph --><p>Ford's advertised base price also excludes <strong>$1,595 destination and delivery</strong>, raising the pre-tax, pre-option vehicle total to <strong>$29,945</strong>. The company expects preorders in early 2027, with availability later that year. Its manufacturing roadmap follows other <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/25/gm-fourth-generation-ev-20-percent-range-lmr-battery-2026/">lower-cost EV platform initiatives</a> and electrification technologies such as <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2026/10/08/honda-wireless-charging-road-150kw-moving-electric-trucks/">wireless truck charging research</a>.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->

Ford's <strong>projected</strong> 10-kW-plus output is a meaningful design target, but owners cannot assume a $28,350 truck alone is a turnkey whole-home power system.</p><!-- /wp:paragraph -->
