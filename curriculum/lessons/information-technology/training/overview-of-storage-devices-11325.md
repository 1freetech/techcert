<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none"><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/20/setting-up-and-configuring-accessories-and-ports-of-mobile-devices/">Storage devices</a> are critical components in computing, used to retain digital information permanently or temporarily. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">The two most common types of internal storage are <strong><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/07/the-role-of-ram-and-rom-in-computer-systems/">Hard Disk Drives</a> (HDDs)</strong> and <strong><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/07/the-role-of-ram-and-rom-in-computer-systems/">Solid-State Drives</a> (SSDs)</strong>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">HDDs use spinning magnetic platters and read/write heads to store data and are valued for their high storage capacities at lower cost per gigabyte. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">However, because they rely on moving parts, HDDs are more prone to mechanical failure and slower in performance compared to solid-state technology. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I0cJLL3I_-k\u0026amp;pp=ygU5My4zIEdpdmVuIGEgc2NlbmFyaW8sIHNlbGVjdCBhbmQgaW5zdGFsbCBzdG9yYWdlIGRldmljZXMu","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">SSDs, on the other hand, use NAND flash memory and have no moving parts, resulting in faster boot times, quicker file access, and improved durability, especially in portable devices like laptops.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://youtu.be/r3Jy5dHOj3g?si=knFIK6D3XZLT-f67","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">External storage options include USB flash drives, external HDDs or SSDs, <a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/01/07/command-8-diskpart-windows-os/">SD cards</a>, and network-attached storage (NAS). These devices are often used for backups, file transfers, or expanding available capacity without opening a system. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Flash drives and SD cards are compact and convenient, but generally have lower capacities and slower performance than internal SSDs. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NgVm2kUmrYo\u0026amp;pp=ygUbT3ZlcnZpZXcgb2YgU3RvcmFnZSBEZXZpY2Vz","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">NAS units are storage systems connected to a network, allowing multiple users or devices to access files remotely. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3yZDDr0JKVc\u0026amp;t=5s\u0026amp;pp=ygUJTkFTIHVuaXRz0gcJCU8JAYcqIYzv","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-4-3 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">These are useful in environments requiring shared access or centralized backup solutions. Most external storage devices connect via USB, Thunderbolt, or eSATA interfaces, which affect data transfer speeds and compatibility.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">When installing a storage device, it's important to choose the correct <strong>form factor</strong> and <strong>interface</strong>. SATA is the most common interface for both HDDs and older SSDs, offering compatibility across a wide range of systems. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=knDVCdfTJiM\u0026amp;pp=ygUbT3ZlcnZpZXcgb2YgU3RvcmFnZSBEZXZpY2Vz","type":"video","providerNameSlug":"youtube","responsive":true,"className":"wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"} -->

</div></figure>
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Newer SSDs may use <strong>NVMe</strong> technology via an <strong>M.2</strong> or <strong>PCIe</strong> connection, delivering significantly faster read/write speeds ideal for gaming, professional content creation, or operating systems. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">In enterprise environments, storage configurations may also include <strong><a href="https://bitcoinversus.tech/2025/03/03/understanding-raid-levels/">RAID</a> arrays</strong> for redundancy and performance or cloud-based storage for scalability and remote access. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"textTransform":"none"}}} -->
<p style="text-transform:none">Understanding the differences in speed, cost, reliability, and capacity helps technicians select the appropriate storage solution for a given scenario.</p>
