## Series means one path
In a simple series circuit, components are connected one after another and current has one path.

For series resistors:
R total = R1 + R2 + R3

## Parallel means multiple paths
Parallel branches provide multiple current paths. Components connected across the same two nodes have the same voltage across those nodes.

Parallel paths can also affect in-circuit resistance measurements.

## Simple example
Two 10-ohm resistors in series = 20 ohms.
Two equal 10-ohm resistors in parallel = 5 ohms.

## Quick practice
1. 2 + 3 + 5 ohms in series = 10 ohms.
2. One continuous path = series.
3. Several branches across the same two nodes = parallel.
4. De-energize before resistance measurement and consider parallel paths.

## OSETC takeaway
Series = one path. Parallel = multiple paths. Recognizing the arrangement helps technicians understand measurements and troubleshoot circuits.
