The Bitaxe mining device has shown a unique performance pattern at startup, achieving hash rates up to 3 TH/s before stabilizing to a lower average of 300 - 400 GH/s.

This surge in efficiency upon startup highlights the initial calibration and thermal conditions affecting mining hardware performance.

Temperature Plays a Role in Efficiency

Temperature plays a significant role in the device’s performance, with optimal hash rates achieved at cooler initial conditions.

As internal temperatures rise, efficiency drops, likely due to thermal throttling, a common effect where devices reduce power output to prevent overheating. Enhanced cooling could prolong high performance.

PSU Stability and Hash Rate Sustainability

Consistent power output is crucial for sustained efficiency. Power fluctuations after startup may contribute to the lower average hash rate seen in Bitaxe devices over time.

Ensuring stable voltage and power input may help maintain performance, though complete stability may require firmware adjustments.
